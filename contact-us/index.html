Contact Us

We'd love to hear from you

Business Inquiries

For business or partnership inquiries, please reach out to us by email at contact@bedrock-cosmos.app. We aim to respond to all business-related messages as promptly as possible.

General Support & Joining the Team

For general support, or if you're interested in joining the Bedrock Cosmos team, the best place to reach us is our Discord Server. Our community and team members are active there and happy to help.

Bug Reports & Contributions

Found a bug, or want to contribute to the project? Bedrock Cosmos is fully open source. Head over to our GitHub page to open an issue or submit a pull request.

Response Times

Bedrock Cosmos is maintained by a small, dedicated team. While we do our best to respond quickly, please allow some time for us to get back to you, especially for detailed bug reports or contribution reviews.
